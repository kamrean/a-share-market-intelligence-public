active state/contract/policy, docs/DAILY_MARKET_EVIDENCE.md, PR11 merge and named
historical prerequisites. Never mutate archived/private projects.

state/log before review. Exactly four current attestations are exempt: bundle,
validation, Reviewer and Release. Every historical receipt is immutable.

identity. Python3.12 stdlib only. Stop on unexpected dirty paths/secrets/scope or
failed gates. Never direct/auto merge, admin bypass or force push.
